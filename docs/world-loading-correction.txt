New-world generation: original corrupt/forest and lower-frame art, gold total
progress and blue current-pass progress. Existing-world entry: centered white
outlined status and percentage. Both retain the active menu's original title,
forest and moving clouds. Removed the extra runner, stars and modern blue panel.

Percentages describe this implementation's actual completed stages, not a fake
copy of Terraria's internal generation passes. Overall progress is finite,
clamped, monotonic. New passes may reset their own lower bar. No fake delay.
The world stays inactive until ready. Saved blocks, inventory and player position
are restored before 100%, fade and autosave. Existing save format is unchanged.
